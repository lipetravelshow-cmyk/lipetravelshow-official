How to build a better city journey

Between museums there is the metro, the next table, a bookshop, a market and streets where everyday life explains the destination better than any checklist.

Replace the checklist with neighbourhoods

Plan each day around nearby districts. One major museum, one meal and unstructured time often beat six scattered attractions.

Give the city time

Three or four nights reveal the surface; five nights allow habits, timing and shifts in atmosphere to emerge.
