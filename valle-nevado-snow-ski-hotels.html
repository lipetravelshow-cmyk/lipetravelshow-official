Valle Nevado: day trip, snow, skiing and where to stay

Valle Nevado can be a single snow day from Santiago or a multi-day mountain trip. The right choice depends on what you actually want to do: touch snow, ride the gondola, ski or wake up in the Andes.

First: what experience do you want?

01

I just want to see snow

You can go up and experience snow without buying a ski pass. A specific ticket is required to ride the gondola or lifts.

02

I want a full mountain day

Choose a dedicated tour or transfer that leaves real time at Valle Nevado. Multi-stop generic mountain tours can make the resort stop too short.

03

I want to sleep in the snow

Best for skiers, families or travelers who want the Andes to become a central part of the trip.

RESIDENCES

Apartments / Ski Residences

Aconcagua Ski Residences and resort apartments suit groups who want more independence. Do not treat them as a fourth hotel: meals, services and lift access may follow different rules.

ROAD

How to cover 60 km without underestimating the mountain

The distance is short, but the road climbs through many mountain curves. In winter, road conditions, ice and chain requirements can change quickly. For most travelers, a specialist transfer or tour is the more predictable choice.
