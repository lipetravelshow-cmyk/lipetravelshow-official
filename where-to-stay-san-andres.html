SAN ANDRES · WHERE TO STAY

Where to stay in San Andres: choose your base by beach and travel pace

North End is easiest for a first visit; San Luis is calmer and beach-focused; La Loma adds culture and a central inland feel; South End slows everything down and puts diving and rocky coastline closer.

SAN ANDRES

Four bases, four ways to experience San Andres

The island is compact, but your base changes how much you walk, use transport, stay close to the beach and join the evening buzz.

01
First trip + convenience

North End / Spratt Bight

The easiest first-time base: urban beach, promenade, restaurants, shopping and many boat departures are nearby.

Focus areaDowntown / Spratt Bight
Best forFirst visit · short stays · travelers who want to walk
MobilityExcellent on foot downtown; transport for south and west
Hotel / strategyAquamare · Casablanca · Bahía Sardina · Sea Avenue
Watch out

This is the busiest and least quiet part of the island.

Click to explore
02
Beach + slower pace

San Luis / Rocky Cay

A quieter east-coast experience with beautiful water and access to Rocky Cay.

Focus areaSan Luis · Rocky Cay
Best forCouples · families · relaxation · slower stays
MobilityTransport matters more; taxi or a one-day vehicle helps
Hotel / strategyCocoplum Beach Hotel
Watch out

Fewer restaurants and shops within walking distance than North End.

Click to explore
03
Raizal culture + inland

La Loma

A more local, elevated and cultural base for travelers who want another layer of island life.

Focus areaLa Loma · First Baptist Church area
Best forCurious travelers · culture · more independent stays
MobilityTaxi or a vehicle helps; this is not a beach base
Hotel / strategyChoose it for atmosphere, not beach convenience
Watch out

Not ideal if your priority is stepping straight onto the sand.

Click to explore
04
Quiet + west/south coast

South End / El Cove

Quieter, more spread out and closer to diving points, rocky coastline and local restaurants.

Focus areaEl Cove · South End
Best forDiving · relaxation · couples · longer stays
MobilityA car, buggy or taxi is useful
Hotel / strategyA strong base when sea and quiet matter more than shopping
Watch out

Farther from Spratt Bight and most nightlife.
