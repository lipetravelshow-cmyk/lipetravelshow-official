BOGOTÁ · WHERE TO STAY

Where to stay in Bogotá: six bases, six ways to experience the city

Bogotá is large, traffic matters and your base changes the daily experience. Decide whether you want history, food, nightlife, comfort in the north or a more central position before comparing room rates.

↓Open hotels →

Choose by the daily routine you want

There is no single best neighborhood. There is the neighborhood that reduces transfers and fits your way of traveling.

01

History + first immersion

La Candelaria

Sleeping inside the historic core makes museums, churches, Plaza de Bolívar and colonial streets easy to reach.

Where to focus

Plaza de Bolívar area · Gold Museum corridor

Best for

Culture-first travelers · short stays · first visit

Mobility

Excellent on foot by day; use taxi/app for evenings and northern neighborhoods

Reference hotel

Hotel de la Opera
Also compare smaller heritage properties in the historic center

Watch forAt night, the exact street and transport plan matter more than in the north.
See recommended hotel →
02

Charm + food

Quinta Camacho / Chapinero

Brick houses, cafés and restaurants create a more local, contemporary base.

Where to focus

Quinta Camacho · Zona G · around Calle 65

Best for

Couples · food-focused travelers · neighborhood atmosphere

Mobility

Balanced by taxi/app for both the center and the north

Reference hotel

HAB Hotel
Another nearby premium reference: Four Seasons Casa Medina

Watch forIt is less touristy than La Candelaria; that local rhythm is the point.
See recommended hotel →
03

Nightlife + shopping

Zona T / Zona Rosa

The most direct base for restaurants, malls and nightlife, with plenty of movement after dinner.

Where to focus

Zona T · Andino · El Retiro

Best for

Nightlife · shopping · first trip focused on the north

Mobility

Easy on foot inside the zone; taxi/app for the historic center

Reference hotel

Sofitel Bogotá Victoria Regia
Also compare La Cabrera and the Calle 85 area

Watch forIt is commercial rather than historic; do not choose it only because the name is famous.
See recommended hotel →
04

Best balance in the north

Parque 93 / Chicó

Modern, greener and predictable for restaurants, cafés and comfortable stays.

Where to focus

Parque 93 · Chicó Norte · Calle 93 corridor

Best for

First trip · work + leisure · couples · families

Mobility

Very good by taxi/app; travel time to the historic center is longer

Reference hotel

Novotel Bogotá Parque 93
For a stronger design angle, compare The Click Clack Hotel Bogotá

Watch forComfort comes with longer transfers when most of your day is concentrated downtown.
See recommended hotel →
05

Charm + slower pace

Usaquén

A former village absorbed by the city, with restaurants, pleasant streets and a Sunday market.

Where to focus

Historic Usaquén · Hacienda Santa Bárbara · Calle 116 area

Best for

Repeat visitors · slow stays · food

Mobility

Convenient within the north; farther from La Candelaria and central museums

Reference hotel

Biohotel Organic Suites
Many modern properties sit between Calle 100 and 120

Watch forNot the most efficient base if you plan two consecutive days in the historic center.
See recommended hotel →
06

Central + culture

Centro Internacional

A bridge between the historic core and contemporary Bogotá, with access to museums, La Macarena and business corridors.

Where to focus

National Museum · Carrera 7 · La Macarena

Best for

Culture · business · travelers who want a central position

Mobility

Good for balancing downtown, the north and the airport without staying at an extreme

Reference hotel

Grand Hyatt Bogotá
Grand Hyatt is farther west; use it as a profile reference rather than exact micro-location

Watch forThe experience changes significantly street by street, so always check the immediate surroundings.
