HOW TO CHOOSE

Two experiences that solve different needs

Aquitania fills a short opening without leaving the city; Concha y Toro turns wine into a half-day excursion. They belong side by side rather than in a winner-takes-all ranking.

Wine tastings are adult experiences; check the provider rules before booking.
