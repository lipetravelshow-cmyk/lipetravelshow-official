How to travel through history without visual fatigue

Old streets are not frozen sets. They hold political choices, trade, conflict, belief and ways of life that remain visible in the present.

Carry context with you

A book, documentary or podcast before each major stop changes what you are able to notice.

Alternate monument and everyday life

After a palace or ruin, walk through a market, neighbourhood or café. That is where the past starts breathing again.
